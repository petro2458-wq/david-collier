About

My story

I'm a petroleum engineer. I spent 23 years in oil and gas, 15 at Hess and 8 at Kosmos Energy, working the Permian Basin, South Louisiana and the Gulf of Mexico, Equatorial Guinea, Ghana, and Norway. I started as a reservoir and production engineer doing hands-on field work and finished as Vice President, Global Production.

At the end of 2019 I left Kosmos on my own terms to trade futures full-time. I worked at it seriously for six years. I had strong periods, including 2025 and early 2026, but the results were not consistent enough to build a long-term career on. I've decided to return to petroleum engineering, where my experience is deepest, and I'm looking for a role where I can contribute for many years.

The time away added two things I bring with me: disciplined risk management under uncertainty, and real hands-on skill building working software with AI tools. Working for myself, I put far more time into AI than most full-time employees could, and I'm looking forward to applying it to engineering work.
